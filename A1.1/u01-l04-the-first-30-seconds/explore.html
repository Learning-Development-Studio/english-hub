01 · Wh- Questions

Who, What, Where, How old + To Be

Choose the right Wh- question for each answer.

Key: 1. What's · 2. Where · 3. Who · 4. How old · 5. What's.
1. "_____ your name?" — "I'm Clara."
2. "_____ are you from?" — "I'm from Lima, Peru."
3. "_____ is she?" — "She's my partner, Elena."
4. "_____ are you?" — "I'm 28."
5. "_____ your job?" — "I'm an engineer."

02 · Questions & Answers

Wh- + is / are

Choose the right question or answer.

Key: 1. Where is she from? · 2. Who are they? · 3. How old is he? · 4. What's your email?

03 · Contact Words

6 Contact & People Words

Type the missing word(s): age, email, phone number, job, friend, partner.

Key: 1. age · 2. email · 3. phone number · 4. job · 5. friend · 6. partner.
1. "What's your __________? — I'm 27."
2. "My __________ is clara@studio.com."
3. "What's your __________? — It's 555-0192."
4. "What's your __________? — I'm a graphic designer."
