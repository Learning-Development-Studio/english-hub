The Job vs. The Human

Titles on a badge, and real people behind them. Say who you are, not just your job.

01 · Warm-Up

Office Tag vs. Real Person

Look at Card A and Card B for Daniel. Click a card, then say 3 short lines with a partner.

1. Setup: Show the two Daniel cards (4–5 min). Ask students to click Card A (Office Tag) or Card B (Real Person).
2. Modeling: Model with [P] only (L01–L02 repertoire): "Hi, I'm Daniel Vega. My office is big and busy. Just call me Dani! I'm from Oaxaca—born and raised! I'm quiet and happy."
3. Check: Pair students for the 3 Quick Questions. Enforce boundary [F]: no action verbs (work, live, have, do) and no Wh- questions (*What do you do?*).
4. Keys: Open-ended personal responses; listen for accurate I'm / Yes, it is / No, it isn't and rising intonation on Yes/No questions.

3 Quick Questions

Say each line with a partner.

  • 1. "Are you from a big, busy city or a small, quiet town?" → "I'm from a ____."
  • 2. "Is your name on a badge at an office or school?" → "Yes, it is!" / "No, it isn't."
  • 3. "Are you quiet, or are you busy and active?" → "I'm ____."

05 · Grammar

a / an + To Be for Jobs

Always use a or an before one job.

1. Setup: Connect back to Elena & Carlos ("I'm an engineer", "I'm an artist", "She's a doctor").
2. Modeling: Emphasize that Spanish omits the article (*I'm teacher*), whereas English requires a / an before singular professions. Enforce [F]: no action verbs and no Wh- questions.
3. Check: Students complete the 4 Natural vs. Broken Choice items (no red highlights on errors—students spot the natural English line).
4. Keys: 1. She's an engineer. · 2. He isn't a doctor. · 3. Are you an artist? · 4. I'm a teacher at a small school.

Use a before a consonant sound (a doctor). Use an before a vowel sound (an artist).

06 · Speaking

Mystery Job Game

Pick a secret card. Your partner asks Yes/No questions to guess your job!

1. Setup: Assign each student a Secret Card (1–4; in 1-on-1, you take a different card). One partner asks Yes/No questions to guess the job and the human detail.
2. Modeling: Demo: "Are you in a hospital? — No, I'm not. — Is your job creative? — Hmm, not very creative, but it pays the bills! — Are you an engineer? — Yes, I am!"
3. Check: Monitor without interrupting; note any missing a/an or Wh- slips for delayed feedback.
4. Keys: Target evidence: accurate Are you a/an...? questions, short answers, and 2+ USA Chunks.

07 · Exit Ticket

My 3 Sentences

Write 3 true sentences about your job (or student life) and a friend.

1. Setup: Give 3–4 minutes for independent writing in the blank box without copying a model.
2. Modeling: Remind students to include: (1) your job/student status with a / an (or between jobs), (2) a friend (he or she) + a / an + job, and (3) one Yes/No question.
3. Check: Students click Check My Sentences, read aloud, and check off the 3 Can-Do statements.
4. Keys: Understanding Comes Before Advancement: advance to A1.1-U01-L04 only when the student achieves E2.
