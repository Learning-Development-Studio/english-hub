The Meaning Behind the Object

A small thing in your bag or on your desk can carry a big story. Talk about the special objects in your life.

01 · Warm-Up

Keep It or Let It Go?

Look at these 3 items. Tap Keep it! or Let it go! and share 1 quick reason with a partner.

1. Setup: Present Keep It or Let It Go? (4–5 min) with the 3 visual item cards (Old family photo, Old dog toy, Grandma's ring). Students tap "Keep it!" or "Let it go!" (no percentages or scores).
2. Modeling: Model 1 quick reason using strictly [P] (To Be, possessive adjectives, Saxon Genitive 's): "It's old, but it's nice!" / "It isn't useful now." Do NOT introduce have / has yet.
3. Check: In pairs, students share 1 quick reason for each choice in [P]. Enforce boundary [F]: NO do/does and NO routine have.
4. Keys: Open-ended personal responses with It's old / nice / special, It's my grandma's, and It isn't useful now.

03 · USA Chunks

3 Useful USA Phrases

Use these 3 phrases as one piece when you talk about a special object.

1. Setup: Present these 3 expressions as ready-to-use conversational blocks [L].
2. Modeling: Play each Phrase and Example audio. Do NOT analyze grammar inside "I can't let it go" or "For old time's sake".
3. Check: Quick choral repetition + mini-exchange: Teacher holds up an object (e.g., a mug or pen) and says "It's old, but it's priceless to me!" → Students echo with their own object.
4. Keys: All 3 chunks appear in The Tiny Museum reading next.

04 · Reading

The Tiny Museum

Welcome to a museum of small, special objects! Tap each case to read its plaque. Then do the True / False fact-check.

1. Setup: Frame it as a museum tour: "Today we visit The Tiny Museum." Students open the 3 exhibit cases and read each plaque silently (4 min), spotting the 3 USA Chunks (priceless to me, for old time's sake, I can't let it go). Play the optional audio only after silent reading.
2. Modeling: Point out have, has, don't have, doesn't have on the plaques as an inductive preview for Grammar.
3. Check: The True / False Fact-Check unlocks after all 3 cases are open. For every False item, students say the correct fact aloud ("No! It's her grandma's ring.").
4. Keys: 1. F (grandma's ring) · 2. T · 3. F (his sister has 500 pictures) · 4. T · 5. F (it's from his first job).

05 · Grammar

Have / Has (My Objects)

Say what objects you and other people have—and what you don't have.

1. Setup: Connect back to the Reading profiles ("I have an old gold ring", "My sister has a new phone", "Leo doesn't have a new mug").
2. Modeling: Highlight affirmative and negative forms ONLY: I / you / we / they + have / don't have and he / she / it + has / doesn't have. Drill the Pronunciation Focus (have /hæv/ vs. has /hæz/). Enforce [F]: NO do/does questions and NO routine have (have breakfast, have fun).
3. Check: Part A: Students tap the 8 toggle pills across the 4 inventory cards and click Check Inventory (X/8). Part B: Students build 2 truths and 1 lie, click Lock & Challenge Partner, and read their 3 sentences aloud. Partners guess using [P] ("Is number 2 the lie?").
4. Keys (Part A): Elena: has / doesn't have · Mateo: has / doesn't have · Sofia & Leo: have / don't have · You (I): have / don't have.

Use have or has for objects with you. Use don't have or doesn't have when an object is not with you.

Pronunciation Focus

Have (/hæv/) vs. Has (/hæz/)

Listen to the final sound: have ends in /v/, and has ends in a buzzing /z/.

have /hæv/ I have a ring. They have a photo.
has /hæz/ She has a letter. He has a mug.

06 · Speaking

Real-Object 45s Spotlight

Show a real object, pick 1 emotional tag, and speak for 45 seconds while your partner checks live.

1. Setup: Ask each student to hold 1 real object from their pocket, desk, or bag, and choose 1 emotional tag ("Priceless to me", "I can't let it go", or "For old time's sake").
2. Modeling: Hold up a real object (e.g., keys or a mug) and model 45s spontaneous speech with no written script on screen.
3. Check: Partners tap the Live Peer Checklist checkboxes as they hear each element in real time (0/4 Checked → 4/4 Checked). Monitor for [F] (no Do/Does questions or routine have) and apply delayed feedback for *She have* or *doesn't has*.
4. Keys: Target evidence: have/has + don't/doesn't have + real object name + chosen emotional tag (4/4 Checked).

07 · Exit Ticket

My Special Object

Write 2 short sentences about an object you or a family member have, and 1 sentence about what you don't have.

1. Setup: Give 3–4 minutes for independent writing in the blank textarea without copying a model.
2. Modeling: Remind students to include: (1) an object with have / has, (2) one negative sentence with don't have / doesn't have, and (3) one USA Chunk if possible.
3. Check: Students click Check My Story, read aloud, and self-verify the 3 Can-Do statements.
4. Keys: Understanding Comes Before Advancement: advance to A1.1-U03-L01 only when the student achieves E2 (accurate have / has / don't have / doesn't have + no routine have or do/does questions).
