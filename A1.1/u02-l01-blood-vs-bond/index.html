Blood vs. Bond

Family by blood, and family by heart. Talk about the people and pets in your life.

01 · Warm-Up

Guess the Bond

Look at the 3 photos. Click what you think the bond is: Family, Friends, or Pet & Human.

1. Setup: Show the 3 real-photo cards (no text on photos) for 4–5 min. Ask students to pick the real bond under each photo (Photo 1: Family / Friends / Roommates; Photo 2: Family / Friends / Couple; Photo 3: Just a Pet / Real Family!).
2. Modeling: Model with [P] only (U01 repertoire): "Look at photo 1. Are they family or friends? Look at photo 3. Is he just a pet or real family?" Do NOT introduce his / her / our / their yet.
3. Check: Pair students for the pair prompt: "Look at photo 1, 2, and 3. Are they family or friends?" Enforce boundary [F]: no have/has, no Saxon Genitive ('s), and no action verbs.
4. Keys: Open-ended guesses; listen for accurate To Be (They're friends / He's family! / Are they a couple?).

Family & Circle Words

Review the family tree, then listen to the 6 new words and examples. Click a card to see the meaning.

05 · Grammar

Possessive Adjectives

Show who a person, pet, or place belongs to.

1. Setup: Connect back to Elena & Lucas ("his dog", "their home", "our families", "her pet").
2. Modeling: Highlight the Spanish interference trap: Spanish su covers his, her, their, your. In English, his = male owner, her = female owner (regardless of the noun after it). Enforce [F]: no mine/yours/hers and no 's.
3. Check: Students tap the interactive pills in Chat Message Repair (no red error highlights) and click Verify Chat to check their X/4 score.
4. Keys: 1. Her (Her hospital is big.) · 2. His (His dog is Milo.) · 3. Our (Our home is warm.) · 4. Their (Their city is Monterrey.)

Use a possessive adjective before a noun (his dog, her sister, our home). Match the word to the person—his for a man/boy, her for a woman/girl.

06 · Speaking

My Inner Circle

Present 2 close people (or 1 person and 1 pet) in your life using his, her, our, or their.

1. Setup: In Build Your Circle, students pick 3 real roles (mom, dad, brother, sister, cousin, close friend, pet) and type 3 names, then click Launch Card.
2. Modeling: Demo a 60-second talk using only the 3 clean Name · Role badges (no written prompts): "This is Valeria, my sister. Her job is busy. And this is Dani, my close friend. He's like family. Our hometown is Oaxaca!"
3. Check: Students press Start 60s and speak spontaneously while partners track the 4 checklist goals. Apply delayed correction for his / her and watch for *have/has* or *'s*.
4. Keys: Target evidence: accurate his / her / our / their + 2+ circle words + 1+ USA Phrase in 3–4 clear sentences.

07 · Exit Ticket

My 3 Sentences

Write 3 true sentences about 2 people or a pet in your inner circle.

1. Setup: Give 3–4 minutes for independent writing in the blank box without copying a model.
2. Modeling: Remind students to include: (1) his or her + a circle word, (2) our or their, and (3) one USA phrase.
3. Check: Students click Check My Sentences, read aloud, and check off the 3 Can-Do statements.
4. Keys: Understanding Comes Before Advancement: advance to A1.1-U02-L02 only when the student achieves E2.
