Starting University

It is the first day. I am a new student. I am at the university.

María · a student
The story

This is María.

Her name is María. She is 19. She is a new student. She is not from this city.

The university is big. Her classroom is big too. Her classes are new. Her backpack is black.

At nine, she is in class. The teacher is nice. Two students are in her group. The group is small. They are friendly.

María is nervous, but she is not alone. This is her first day. It is a good day.
