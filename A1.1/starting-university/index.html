Starting University

It is the first day. I am a new student. I am at the university. I am nervous, and I am happy too.

“I am new. The classes are not easy. I am okay.”

María · a student
The story

This is María.

She is 19. She is a new student. The university is big. Her classes are new. She is not from this city.

At nine, she is in class. The teacher is nice. Two students are in her group. They are friendly.

María is nervous, but she is not alone. This is her first day. It is a good day.

Notice

I am / She is / They are / It is
Words: university · class · nervous · friendly

Think

  1. Is María a new student? How do you know?
  2. Is she alone in the class?
  3. How is her first day — only bad, or good too?
