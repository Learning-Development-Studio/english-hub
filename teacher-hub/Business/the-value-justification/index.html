Lesson Plan: The Value Justification 1-on-1 (Defending ROI & Impact)

Comprehensive faculty guide to facilitate executive scope defense, quantifiable ROI articulation, and title/remuneration realignment using the functional contrast between Past Simple and Present Perfect.

Course & Unit Track 5 · People & HR (BE-05.2)
CEFR Profile B1.2 → B2 (Executive General English)
Recommended Duration 60 Minutes (Executive Seminar)
Methodology CLT + Task-Based Language Teaching (TBLT)

1. Target Communicative Outcome & Linguistic Inventory (P / P? / N / F)

Observable Outcome: The student crafts and verbally defends a 3-step executive Value Pitch connecting a finished past milestone with continuous compounding business return and a diplomatic alignment ask.

Category Description Classroom Linguistic Inventory
P (Prerequisites) Prior master patterns Past Simple regular & irregular forms (led, launched, completed), basic workplace lexicon (team, client, budget, timeline).
P? (To Confirm) Phonetic & morphological watchpoints Pronunciation of regular past -ed endings (/t/, /d/, /ɪd/), oral fluency with large currency figures and percentages in USD.
N (New Repertoire) Explicit instructional focus The Time-Impact Formula: Past Simple milestone + Present Perfect continuous return + Causal connectors (Given that, Considering that, Now that) + Polite modal ask (would like to align).
F (Forbidden / Off-limits) Do not demand or penalize Mixed Conditionals (If I had done... I would be...), complex inverted conditionals (Had I known...).

2. 60-Minute Pedagogical Architecture

00 – 10 min

Phase 1: Engage & Mindset Diagnostic

Frame the core dilemma: "Why does personal effort consistently fail in compensation discussions?" Guide the learner to contrast Pitch A (emotional effort) vs Pitch B (executive ROI standard). Administer the interactive Diagnostic Poll on the platform.

Mediation Prompt: "If a vendor charges you 20% more because their own operating rent increased, do you automatically accept the invoice? How does executive leadership view our compensation in the exact same light?"
10 – 25 min

Phase 2: Notice & Bimodal Auditory Contrast

Play the studio recordings of Carlos (Pitch A) and Elena (Pitch B). Break down the acoustic differences in pacing, vocal posture, and linguistic precision. Highlight how Elena uses completed (Past Simple) for the finished milestone and has cut / has saved (Present Perfect) for ongoing value.

Attention Focus: Have students pinpoint the specific words that project strategic ownership versus victimhood.
25 – 38 min

Phase 3: Notice & Grammar Architecture (The 3-Step Formula)

Diagram the 3-step Time-Impact Formula on the shared board: (1) Finished Milestone (Past Simple) → (2) Ongoing Compounding Return (Present Perfect) → (3) Diplomatic Scope Alignment Ask. Drill the causal connectors (Given that, Considering that, Now that).

38 – 48 min

Phase 4: Practice & Metric Transformation Arena

Guide students through the interactive challenges on Tab [04], converting weak task descriptions into high-leverage ROI formulations. Validate formulations using the real-time diagnostic engine.

48 – 60 min

Phase 5: Perform & Apply (The Autonomous Pitch Studio)

Each student drafts their authentic Value Pitch on Tab [05] using real-world job responsibilities. They utilize the Listen to My Custom Pitch synthesizer to practice cadenced delivery, followed by a live 1-on-1 negotiation roleplay with the instructor (acting as VP/Director).

Instructor Roleplay Tactic: Present a realistic corporate pushback ("We are currently in a very tight budget cycle") to evaluate whether the learner diplomatically reiterates their ROI.

3. Executive Acronym Reference for Faculty

Acronym Full English Term Classroom Explanation & Context
ROI Return on Investment The measurable financial gain or cost reduction generated by the employee's operational work.
ARR Annual Recurring Revenue Predictable contracted yearly revenue from recurring subscription/client accounts. Key enterprise metric.
MRR Monthly Recurring Revenue Predictable monthly recurring revenue generated from active contracts.
SLA Service Level Agreement Contractual commitment outlining delivery timelines and uptime standards.
Turnaround Turnaround Time Total elapsed time required to process a project from initial request to delivery.

4. Master Executive Connector Bank (Faculty Reference)

Negotiation Stage Connector Communicative Function Model Utterance
Objective Premise Given that... Establishes a proven operational fact "Given that I have assumed full operational ownership..."
Objective Premise Considering that... Analytical and highly diplomatic "Considering that our department has expanded ARR by 18%..."
Objective Premise In light of [results]... Anchors directly to empirical evidence "In light of these continuous results, I would like to..."
Objective Premise Now that... Natural, decisive, and authoritative "Now that I have stabilized our regional delivery team..."
Ongoing Return Since that rollout... Marks the start of active benefit "Since that deployment, this system has cut errors by 35%."
Ongoing Return Over the past [months]... Covers continuous operational duration "Over the past six months, we have protected 140,000 USD."
Ongoing Return To date... Cumulative impact up to present day "To date, this framework has prevented all critical SLA defaults."
Direct Impact Thereby + [ing]... Highlights executive cause-and-effect "I restructured workflows, thereby saving 80,000 USD annually."
Diplomatic Ask I would like to align... Polite negotiation closure "I would like to align my compensation with this expanded scope."

5. Qualitative Evaluation Rubric (E0 – E3)

Band Performance Descriptor Observable Classroom Evidence
E0 (No Evidence) Unable to articulate business value Learner relies entirely on personal living costs (rent, hours worked) without data or commercial leverage.
E1 (Emergent) Identifies project but confuses tenses Uses Present Perfect for fixed past dates ("Last month I have finished") or adopts an aggressive posture ("I want a raise").
E2 (Competent) Accurately executes 3-part formula Articulates with precision: Past Simple milestone + Present Perfect continuous return + Causal connector with modal ask.
E3 (Autonomous) Fluid executive poise under pushback Defends stance calmly, reiterates quantifiable metrics when challenged by leadership, and maintains strategic partner posture.
