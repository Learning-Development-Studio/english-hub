Teacher plan · games first

Habits talk. Make the -s fight fair.

Warm-up races, verb steal, three short grammar hits, then platform drills. Keep score You vs The Exam if you want energy.

≈ 55–60 min 1:1 or small group Notice → Grammar → Practice Optional: Simple Present Battle

Aim

Use Simple Present affirmative & negative (I/you/we/they + base; he/she/it + -s/-es; don’t / doesn’t + base) to talk about daily habits — with laughs, not worksheets.

  • Can-do: Talk about daily habits in affirmative and negative Simple Present.
  • Materials: Student page + phone/laptop; optional board for races.
  • Optional battle (game, not the plan): Simple Present Battle as cool-down / energy high option.

0 · Warm-up 5–7 min Game

Habit Sprint — activate daily-life English before the page.

  1. Timer 40 seconds: “Name things you do every day — GO.” They shout / type a messy list.
  2. You write verbs fast in two columns (no labels yet): I/you/we/they side vs he/she/it side.
  3. Sort shout: point at a verb — they yell BASE! or -S! for “she ___”.
Line: “Today habits talk. Some subjects take the base. One group takes the -s. Let’s make them behave.”
Optional score: +1 You for each correct shout in an 8-verb blitz

1 · Vocabulary 6 min Notice

Meet the high-frequency verb set — choral + flash.

Core verbs

make · use · call · play · need · live · believe · work · study · like

Habit frames

every day · usually · on Mondays · after work · with friends · from home

  1. Open 1 Build the pattern. You point; they chorus the example lines.
  2. Odd one out (oral): “I make / We need / She call / They play” → they spot She call and say why.

2 · Vocab activity 8–10 min Notice Game

Verb Steal + pattern memory.

  1. In 1 Build the pattern, tap each card; student reads the example like a radio ad (fun voice).
  2. Pattern Memory (60s): hide the page. They rewrite 4 mini-sentences (2 affirmative + 2 negative). Reopen — +1 You per sentence correct.
  3. Verb Steal: they add ONE real habit to each subject group + invent a negative twin with don’t/doesn’t.
Page anchors: We need more time · We don’t need more time · She calls her friend · She doesn’t call her friend

3 · Grammar presentation 1 7 min Grammar Showdown

-s Showdown — two subject teams on the board.

  1. Stay on 1 Build the pattern; focus the two big cards.
  2. Board:

I / You / We / They

base verb · don’t + base
I work · We don’t use the car

He / She / It

verb-s / -es · doesn’t + base
She makes breakfast · He doesn’t play

Concept duel: You say a wrong phrase (She make / He don’t like / They lives). They buzz and fix it. +1 You per clean fix.

4 · Grammar presentation 2 7–8 min Grammar Fire round

Affirm / Neg Fire round — flip the polarity fast.

  • Affirmative: subject + right form · Leo makes breakfast.
  • Negative: don’t / doesn’t + base · Sara doesn’t believe that.
  • Watch the trap: doesn’t makes ✗ → doesn’t make ✓
Fire round: You throw a subject + verb; they answer only affirmative OR only negative on your signal — 12 flips, no thinking time.

5 · Grammar presentation 3 5–6 min Grammar Trap hunt

Don’t Trap Hunt — classic don’t/doesn’t and -s mistakes.

  1. Preview traps orally: He don’t… / She not use… / They lives…
  2. Trap hunt: you read a wrong sentence; they slap the table and repair it (same spirit as page 3 Fix the sentence).
  3. Quick repair race: 5 wrong lines → 5 clean rewrites on the board or chat.
Model traps from the page: He don’t play tennis. · She not use a laptop. · They lives near here.

6 · Drill 12–15 min Practice 2–7 Stations

Platform practice as timed mini-games — map to student page sections.

  1. Open the student page. Run stations:
  • 2 Choose the correct form — 3 min sprint
  • 3 Fix the sentence — 4 min
  • 4 Reading: Daniel’s busy week — T/F + 1 discuss question — 4 min
  • 6–7 Speaking card + 30-second verb sprint — 4–5 min (or swap 5 Find someone who… in groups)
  1. After each block: one miss → they read the why out loud (teacher doesn’t lecture).
  2. First-try correct answers = points for You.
Cool-down options: 8 Two truths and a lie (exit energy) OR Simple Present Battle for 8–10 min if energy is high — labeled game, not the lesson plan.

Exit ticket 2 min

  1. 3 lines about THEIR day: 2 affirmative (mix I + he/she) + 1 negative with don’t OR doesn’t.
  2. You only mark form wins/fails — celebrate the -s and the base-after-doesn’t wins.

Timing

Warm-up 5–7 · Vocab 6 · Vocab game 8–10 · Grammar 1–3 ≈ 20 · Drill 12–15 · Exit 2 → ~55–60 min. Cut Reading discuss or Trap hunt repair race if short.
