Teacher plan · games first

Own set two. Snap, guess, ask.

Memorization day for the next 30 high-frequency verbs — not deep analysis. Verb Sprint, Flip Race, Right Verb, Collocation Snap, Hot Seat — then drill stations that drive the deck (Two jobs, Ask me, What happens next?, put/keep/leave/bring).

≈ 55 min 1:1 or small group Deck → Race → Stations 30 high-frequency verbs

Aim

Lock in set 2 · 30 high-frequency verbs (photo → word → sound) so students can choose, collocate, ask with, and act them fast — ready for Simple Present next.

  • Can-do: Recognize and produce these verbs from a photo; pick the right verb in close pairs; start short questions with them.
  • Materials: Student page (Verb deck + games) on phone/laptop; optional board for Sprint / clues.
  • Focus: memorization & approach — keep grammar light (one short hit only).

0 · Warm-up 5–7 min Game

Verb Sprint + Mime warm-up — body and voice before the deck.

  1. Verb Sprint (40s): “Name verbs you use every day — GO.” Messy list on the board (make, say, get, think, call…).
  2. Mime warm-up: you mime 4–5 from the set (make · call · play · put · leave). They shout the verb — no Spanish.
  3. Circle 6 verbs from their list that match set 2. Tease: “Today we own the next thirty.”
Line: “No essays today. Photo → verb → yours. Flip it, snap it, ask with it.”
Optional: +1 You per clean mime guess in under 3 seconds

1 · Vocabulary 8–10 min Notice Flip Race

Open the Verb deck — choral + flip race through a chunk of the 30.

Core slice (examples)

make · say · get · know · think · see · want · use · find · tell · ask · feel · try · leave · put

Also in the deck

mean · keep · let · begin · seem · show · hear · play · move · live · believe · bring · happen · call · need

  1. Open Verb deck. Photo first — they guess; then flip for word + audio.
  2. Choral: they say the verb with the audio (2× each card for the first 10–12).
  3. Flip Race: you show photo only; race to say the verb before the flip. Wrong = teacher flips and they echo once.
Teacher cue: Space = flip · arrows = next/prev · keep the same side when racing through cards.

2 · Vocab activity 10–12 min Notice Game

Right verb + Collocation snap as classroom races + a quick Hot seat teaser.

  1. Right verb: open the activity. Classroom mode — they shout / tap the verb that fits the stem. Run 1 full set of 10. Argue once on a miss, then move on.
  2. Collocation snap: verb shows; race to tap what goes with it (keep a secret, bring your book…). Score Class hits aloud.
  3. Hot seat teaser (2–3 items): read the three clues out loud; they guess before options. Save a full run for stations.
Energy line: “Right verb, snap the partner, steal the clue. Same 30 — different weapons.”
Keep a sticky score: Right hits · Snap hits · Hot seat guesses

3 · Grammar hit 4–5 min Grammar

One short bridge — these verbs will feed Simple Present / questions later. Do not unpack -s / don’t today.

  1. Board two model lines only: I need help. · What do you want?
  2. Students pick any 3 deck verbs and say one short question or I-sentence each (base form is fine).
  3. Tease the special four: put · keep · leave · bring — “same family, different job. Stations will train them.”
Keep it tiny: verb ownership now → habit & question sentences soon. No trap hunt, no -s duel today.

4 · Drill stations 14–16 min Practice Stations

Platform drills as timed mini-games — map to student activities (don’t rename the lesson after them).

  • Right verb — 3 min sprint (second pass if time)
  • Collocation snap — 1 full set · chase a personal best
  • Two jobs — same meaning or two different jobs? · check misses aloud
  • Ask me — 10s question race · shout before the timer
  • What happens next? — situation → verb · 1 set
  • Hot seat — clues first, options second
  • Put · keep · leave · bring — keys/bags/cake situations
  • Can you…? — honest ticks + one short example
  1. Rotate: ~2–3 min per station (skip a second Right / Snap pass if short).
  2. After Two jobs or Put/keep/leave/bring: they fix one miss out loud.
  3. End with Can you…? checkboxes — honest ticks only.
Teacher stay-out: don’t lecture meanings. Photo + audio + repetition does the work.

Exit ticket 3–4 min

  1. They choose 5 verbs they own from the deck.
  2. One simple sentence or question each: I keep a secret. · Do you need help?
  3. You only mark ownership + clarity — celebrate verbs they can say without looking.

Timing

Warm-up 5–7 · Deck / Flip Race 8–10 · Right + Snap + Hot teaser 10–12 · Grammar hit 4–5 · Stations 14–16 · Exit 3–4 → ~55 min. Cut a station or second Right/Snap run if short.
