Teacher plan · games first

Ask clean. Answer short.

Question Sprint, Do/Does Fire, Short-Answer Showdown, Trap Hunt — then Listening (answer→question), Reading, Hot seat. Affirmative & negative stay in the rearview; today is questions only.

≈ 55–60 min 1:1 or small group Notice → Grammar → Practice After: Simple Present Verbs

Aim

Form Yes/No questions with do / does + base, give short answers (Yes, I do / No, she doesn’t), and use light WH (What / Where / When) without re-teaching full affirmative & negative.

  • Can-do: Ask real-life habit questions and answer them clearly in short form.
  • Materials: Student page + phone/laptop; optional board for races.
  • Bridge: Mentally link after Simple Present Verbs (aff/neg) — don’t reteach the whole chart.

0 · Warm-up 5–7 min Game

Question Sprint — activate question energy before the page.

  1. Timer 40 seconds: “Throw me Yes/No questions about my morning — GO.” They fire messy questions at you.
  2. You catch 6–8 on the board (don’t correct yet). Circle every do / does that appeared.
  3. Sort shout: point at a subject — they yell DO! or DOES! for the question helper.
Line: “Yesterday we made statements. Today we flip them — do/does + base, then short answers. Keep the verb calm.”
Optional score: +1 You for each clean DO/DOES shout in an 8-subject blitz

1 · Vocabulary 6 min Notice

Question stems + short-answer frames — choral.

Question helpers

Do you…? · Does he…? · Does she…? · Do they…? · What do…? · Where does…? · When do…?

Short answers

Yes, I do. · No, I don’t. · Yes, she does. · No, he doesn’t. · Yes, they do. · No, it doesn’t.

  1. Open the Notice tab. Point; they chorus examples.
  2. Odd one out (oral): “Do you work / Does she live / Do he play / Does it start” → they spot Do he play and say why.

2 · Vocab activity 8–10 min Notice Game

Do/Does Fire + pattern memory.

  1. In the Notice tab, tap each card; student reads like a radio host.
  2. Pattern Memory (60s): hide the page. They rewrite 4 mini-questions (2 Do + 2 Does) + matching short answers. Reopen — +1 You per clean pair.
  3. Do/Does Fire: you throw a subject + verb; they fire the full Yes/No question in under 3 seconds.
Page anchors: Do you work on Saturdays? · Yes, I do. · Does Elena start at eight? · No, he doesn’t. · What do you study?

3 · Grammar presentation 1 7 min Grammar Showdown

Short-Answer Showdown — Yes/No teams on the board.

  1. Open the Grammar tab; lock the two big cards.
  2. Board:

Do + I / you / we / they

+ base · Yes, … do / No, … don’t
Do they need help? · Yes, they do.

Does + he / she / it

+ base · Yes, … does / No, … doesn’t
Does Kenji play? · No, he doesn’t.

Showdown: You ask a rapid Yes/No question. They answer ONLY with the short form — no full sentence. Wrong helper or wrong base = miss.

4 · Grammar presentation 2 7–8 min Grammar Fire round

Do/Does Fire round — flip statement → question fast.

  • Statement → question: Elena starts at eight. → Does Elena start at eight?
  • Keep the base: after do/does, no -s. Does he likes? ✗
  • Light WH add-on: What do you study? Where does Omar live?
Fire round: You throw a short affirmative; they return the question + one short answer on your signal — 12 flips.

5 · Grammar presentation 3 5–6 min Grammar Trap hunt

Trap Hunt — Does he likes? / Do she…? / wrong short answers.

  1. Preview traps orally: Does he likes coffee? / Do she work? / Does they live…? / Yes, she do.
  2. Trap hunt: you read a wrong question; they slap the table and repair it (same spirit as the Practice tab · Fix the question).
  3. Quick repair race: 5 wrong lines → 5 clean rewrites on the board or chat.
Model traps from the page: Does he likes coffee? · Does they live in León? · Do she work from home? · What does you need? · Does it starts at nine?

6 · Drill 12–15 min Practice · Listening · Reading · Speaking Stations

Platform practice as timed mini-games — map to student page sections.

  1. Open the student page. Run stations:
  • Practice — Choose the question + Fix traps — 2–3 min sprint
  • Listening — Nadia’s interview — play audio; answer→question, then T/F — 4–5 min
  • Reading — Amira’s first week — main idea + inference + write-the-question — 3–4 min
  • Speaking — Find someone who… + Hot seat / Speed ask — 4–5 min
  • Writing — exit ticket + can-do (optional if time)
  1. After each block: one miss → they say the why out loud (teacher doesn’t lecture).
  2. First-try correct answers = points for You.
Listening tip: Play the audio first. Script button stays under the audio bar — keep it closed until Part A is done.
Cool-down: Speaking tab — Hot seat (60–90s) then Speed ask with the timer — or return to Short-Answer Showdown for a final 8-question blitz.

Exit ticket 2 min

  1. 3 lines: 2 Yes/No questions (mix Do + Does) + 1 short answer pair about THEIR week.
  2. You only mark form wins/fails — celebrate base-after-does and matching short answers.

Timing

Warm-up 5–7 · Vocab 6 · Vocab game 8–10 · Grammar 1–3 ≈ 20 · Drill 12–15 · Exit 2 → ~55–60 min. Cut Find someone who or Trap hunt repair race if short; keep Listening answer→question.
