Adverbs of Frequency

Teacher Hub · dynamic class plan

Teacher plan · games first

How often? Say it clearly.

Frequency Line-up, Odd adverb out, Position Fire, Trap Hunt — then Listening (Remy’s week), Reading, How often speaking, Find someone who, Can-do. No Two truths / one lie. No Sprint warm-up clone.

≈ 55–60 min 1:1 or small group Notice → Grammar → Practice After: Simple Present Questions

Aim

Use the scale always → usually → often → sometimes → rarely / seldom → never; place adverbs before the main verb and after be; ask and answer with How often…?

  • Can-do: Describe real habits with the right adverb and position; ask How often questions.
  • Materials: Student page + phone/laptop; floor/wall space for the line-up; optional sticky notes.
  • Bridge: After Simple Present Questions — keep do/does calm; today is frequency + position.

0 · Warm-up 5–7 min Game

Frequency Line-up — bodies on a never→always line (NOT a naming sprint).

  1. Clear a line on the floor / wall: left = never, right = always. Mark middle = sometimes.
  2. Call a habit: “drink coffee in the morning” · “study English on Sundays” · “be late to class”. Students stand where their truth is.
  3. Spot-check 3 people: they must say a full sentence with the adverb in the right place (I rarely drink coffee. / I’m sometimes late.).
  4. Optional twist: you stand on the line for a fake habit; they guess your adverb before you speak.
Line: “Today isn’t a list race. Your feet show how often — then your mouth puts the adverb in the right seat.”
Optional score: +1 You for each clean position after be vs before verb

1 · Vocabulary 6 min Notice

Scale + position frames — choral.

Scale (0% → 100%)

never · rarely / seldom · sometimes · often · usually · always

Position

I usually cook. · She is always kind. · How often do you…? · How often are you…?

  1. Open the Notice tab. Point at the scale; they chorus left→right.
  2. Odd adverb out (oral): “always / usually / banana / never” or “I drink usually tea / I usually drink tea / She is always early” — they spot the odd/wrong one and say why.

2 · Vocab activity 8–10 min Notice Game

Mime the habit + guess how often + pattern memory.

  1. One student mimes a habit (cook, study, sleep late). Class asks How often do you…? Mime-student answers with one adverb only first, then a full sentence.
  2. In the Notice tab, tap each card; student reads like a radio host.
  3. Pattern Memory (60s): hide the page. They rewrite 2 before-verb + 2 after-be sentences. Reopen — +1 You per clean pair.
Page anchors: I usually drink tea. · He is always early. · How often does Remy go to the gym? · They never eat fish.

3 · Grammar presentation 1 7 min Grammar Showdown

Position Showdown — before verb vs after be.

  1. Open the Grammar tab; lock the two big cards.
  2. Board:

Before main verb

subject + adverb + verb
Yuki usually walks. · They never eat meat.

After be

subject + be + adverb
Nora is always kind. · We are sometimes busy.

Showdown: You throw a subject + adverb + cue (verb OR be). They return ONE clean sentence. Wrong seat = miss.

4 · Grammar presentation 2 7–8 min Grammar Fire round

How often Fire — question ↔ answer fast.

  • Question: How often do you cook? → I often cook on Sundays.
  • With be: How often are you late? → I’m sometimes late on Mondays.
  • Keep do/does for habits; keep be for states.
Fire round: You throw a prompt card (cook / tea / late / gym). They ask How often + answer with a real adverb — 10 swaps.

5 · Grammar presentation 3 5–6 min Grammar Trap hunt

Trap Hunt — I drink usually… / She always is… / broken How often.

  1. Preview traps orally: I wake usually up early. / She often is tired. / How often you study?
  2. Trap hunt: you read a wrong line; they slap the table and repair it (same spirit as the Practice tab · Fix the sentence).
  3. Quick repair race: 5 wrong lines → 5 clean rewrites on the board or chat.
Model traps from the page: I wake usually up early. · She often is tired after work. · They drink never coffee. · How often you study English? · We always are happy to help. · He cooks sometimes pasta.

6 · Drill 12–15 min Practice · Listening · Reading · Speaking Stations

Platform practice as timed mini-stations — map to student page sections.

  1. Open the student page. Run stations:
  • Practice — Choose the form + Fix traps — 2–3 min
  • Listening — Remy’s week — play audio first; Part A then inference T/F — 4–5 min
  • Reading — Nora’s note — main idea + inference + How often write — 3–4 min
  • Speaking — How often interview + Find someone who… — 4–5 min
  • Writing — exit ticket + can-do (optional if time)
  1. After each block: one miss → they say the why out loud (teacher doesn’t lecture).
  2. First-try correct answers = points for You.
Listening tip: Play the audio bar first. Show script button stays below the audio — keep it closed until Part A is done. Placeholder mp3 is fine until real audio lands.
Cool-down: Return to Frequency Line-up for 2 final habits, or the Writing tab can-do prompts.

Exit ticket 2 min

  1. 3 lines: 1 before-verb · 1 after-be · 1 How often question about THEIR week.
  2. You only mark form wins/fails — celebrate adverb seat + matching How often.

Timing

Warm-up 5–7 · Vocab 6 · Vocab game 8–10 · Grammar 1–3 ≈ 20 · Drill 12–15 · Exit 2 → ~55–60 min. Cut Find someone who or Trap hunt repair race if short; keep Listening inference.
