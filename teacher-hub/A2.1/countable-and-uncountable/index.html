Countable & Uncountable

Teacher Hub · dynamic class plan

Teacher plan · games first

Open the fridge. Make the words fight fair.

Warm-up races, vocab games, three short grammar hits, then platform drills. Keep score You vs The Fridge if you want energy.

≈ 55 min 1:1 or small group Notice → Grammar → Practice Optional: Fridge Battle

Aim

Sort kitchen nouns into countable / uncountable and use some / any / much / many / a few / a little / a lot of about the fridge — with laughs, not worksheets.

  • Can-do: Talk about food at home with the right quantity words.
  • Materials: Student page + phone/laptop; optional board for races.
  • Optional battle: Fridge Battle as cool-down.

0 · Warm-up 5–7 min Game

Fridge Sprint — activate kitchen English before the page.

  1. Timer 40 seconds: “Name foods in your fridge — GO.” They shout / type a list.
  2. You write words fast in two messy columns (no labels yet).
  3. Sort shout: point at a word — they yell COUNT! or MEASURE!
Line: “Today the fridge talks. Some words you count. Some words you measure. Let’s make them behave.”
Optional score: +1 You for each correct shout in a 8-word blitz

1 · Vocabulary 6 min Notice

Meet the lesson set — choral + flash.

Countable

eggs · tortillas · tomatoes · bottles

Uncountable

rice · milk · salsa · cheese

  1. Open 1 Notice. You point; they chorus the word.
  2. Odd one out (oral): “eggs, tortillas, milk, tomatoes” → they spot milk and say why.

2 · Vocab activity 8–10 min Notice Game

Tap & Steal + Fridge Memory.

  1. In Notice, tap each food; student reads the sentence like a radio ad (fun voice).
  2. Fridge Memory (60s): hide the page. They rewrite 4 sentences (2+2). Reopen — +1 You per sentence correct.
  3. Steal a food: they add ONE food from their real fridge to each column + invent a sentence with some/any/a few/a little.
Page sentences: three eggs · a few tortillas · How many tomatoes… · three bottles · some rice · How much milk… · a little salsa · don’t have any cheese

3 · Grammar presentation 1 7 min Grammar Showdown

Two kinds of nouns — board showdown.

  1. Open 2 Grammar; focus only the two big cards first.
  2. Board:

Countable

a/an · -s · how many
an egg · two eggs · a few tortillas

Uncountable

no a/an · no plural · how much
some rice · a little salsa

Concept duel: You say a wrong phrase (two rices / a milk / how many cheese). They buzz and fix it. +1 You per clean fix.

4 · Grammar presentation 2 7–8 min Grammar Fire round

Quantifiers — contrast pairs.

  • some → yes-sentences · We have some cheese.
  • any → questions / negatives · Do you have any salsa?
  • many / a few → countable
  • much / a little → uncountable
  • a lot of → both (escape hatch)
Fire round: You throw a noun; they answer only with How many? or How much? — 12 nouns, no thinking time.

5 · Grammar presentation 3 5–6 min Grammar Trap hunt

Classic traps + containers.

  1. Show wrong/right on the page (informations / two breads / How many milk).
  2. Trap hunt (oral): you read a wrong sentence; they slap the table and repair it.
  3. Containers race: bottle / loaf / cup / bag / piece — they build 5 phrases for their kitchen.

6 · Drill 12–15 min Practice A–D Stations

Platform practice as timed mini-games.

  1. Open 3 Practice. Run stations:
  • A Countable or uncountable? — 3 min sprint
  • B Choose the word — 4 min
  • C Fix the sentence — 4 min
  • D A bottle of… — 3 min
  1. After each block: one miss → they read the why out loud (teacher doesn’t lecture).
  2. First-try correct answers = points for You.
Cool-down options: Listening tab (fridge audio) OR Fridge Battle for 8–10 min if energy is high.

Exit ticket 2 min

  1. 3 lines about THEIR fridge with: some/any + a few OR a little + how much OR how many.
  2. You only mark quantity-word wins/fails — celebrate the wins.

Timing

Warm-up 5–7 · Vocab 6 · Vocab game 8–10 · Grammar 1–3 ≈ 20 · Drill 12–15 · Exit 2 → ~55–60 min. Cut Grammar 3 containers if short.
