Aim

Sort fridge nouns into countable / uncountable, use some / any / much / many / a few / a little / a lot of, then say full kitchen chunks (a carton of milk) and finish with Fridge Battle.

  • Can-do: Talk about food at home with the right quantity words and natural container phrases.

Block A Countable / Uncountable ~45–50 min

Core grammar day on the countable page — then a short Reading + Listening hit before chunks.

A0 · Warm-up 5–6 min Odd food out

Odd food out — not a fridge sprint. Activate countable vs uncountable with judgment, not listing.

  1. Board (or say aloud): apple · rice · milk · banana.
  2. Students pick the odd one(s) out and must say why using countable / uncountable language.
  3. Accept strong answers: rice and milk are uncountable; apple and banana are countable. Push: “How many apples?” vs “How much rice?”
  4. Fast round 2 (oral only): eggs / cheese / tortillas / salsa — same job.
Line: “Don’t list your fridge today. Make the words fight: which ones you count, which ones you measure.”
+1 You for each clear why (countable / uncountable), not just the word pick

A1 · Notice 6–7 min Page 1 · 1 Notice

Open: Countable & Uncountable → tab 1 Notice

Meet the fridge set — choral + flash sentences.

Countable

eggs · tortillas · tomatoes · bottles

Uncountable

rice · milk · salsa · cheese

  1. You point; they chorus each food + the page sentence (radio-ad voice OK).
  2. Hide page 20s: they recall 2 countable + 2 uncountable sentences.

A2 · Grammar hits 12–14 min Page 1 · 2 Grammar Fire + traps

Open: same page → tab 2 Grammar

Countable / uncountable cards + quantifiers: some / any / much / many / a few / a little (+ a lot of).

  1. Hit 1 (4 min): two big cards — countable = a/an · -s · how many; uncountable = no plural · how much. Wrong-phrase duel: two rices / a milk / how many cheese → they fix.
  2. Hit 2 (5 min): contrast pairs — some (yes) · any (Q/neg) · many / a few (C) · much / a little (U) · a lot of (both). Fire round: you throw a noun → only How many? or How much?
  3. Hit 3 (3–4 min): traps + containers on the page (informations / two breads / How many milk) → slap-table repair; quick bottle / loaf / cup / bag / piece phrases.

A3 · Practice stations 12–14 min Page 1 · 3 Practice Stations

Open: tab 3 Practice · accordions A–D
  • A Countable or uncountable? — 3 min sprint
  • B Choose the word (some/any/much/many/a few/a little) — 4 min
  • C Fix the sentence — 4 min
  • D A bottle of… — 3 min
  1. After each block: one miss → they read the why out loud (you don’t lecture).
  2. First-try correct = points for You.

A4 · Reading 5–6 min Page 1 · 4 Reading Short-path cut

Open: tab 4 Reading
  1. Silent skim once; then tap/check the highlighted quantity words together.
  2. One oral: “Find one some/any and one how much / how many idea in the text.”

A5 · Listening 5–6 min Page 1 · 5 Listening Short-path cut

Open: tab 5 Listening · After work, tacos
  1. Play once for gist (who / what food).
  2. Play again for quantity details; check on-page answers.
  3. Bridge line: “Next we stop saying just milk — we say the whole chunk.”

Block B Food quantity chunks ~25–30 min

Switch to Kitchen words / Food Quantifiers — whole phrases, not single nouns.

B1 · Phrase deck 5 min Page 2 · 01 Phrase deck Present

Open: Food Quantifiers → accordion 01 Phrase deck (#act-present)
  1. Projector: show photo → wait → students say the chunk → flip.
  2. Run ~8–10 cards; 🔊 for model when they stall.
  3. Rule: full phrase only (a carton of milk, not milk).

B2 · Phrase wall 3–4 min Page 2 · 02 Phrase wall Short-path cut

Open: accordion 02 Phrase wall (#act-wall)
  1. Quick gallery: teal = a ___ of containers; navy = quantity phrases.
  2. Tap 4–6 photos; students echo before audio if possible.

B3 · Container snap 5 min Page 2 · 03 Container snap Snap

Open: accordion 03 Container snap (#act-snap)
  1. Tap phrase → tap photo (or drag). Aim for a clean round (0→8).
  2. Miss → say the correct chunk aloud before next try.

B4 · Memory 4–5 min Page 2 · 04 Memory Short-path cut

Open: accordion 04 Memory (#act-mem) · 8 pairs
  1. Photo ↔ full phrase. Timed pairs race if energy is high.
  2. On match: student uses the chunk in one new sentence.

B5 · Close call 4–5 min Page 2 · 05 Close call Trap

Open: accordion 05 Close call (#act-qty)
  1. Four almost-right phrases; tap the one that matches the photo amount.
  2. Run ~6–8 pictures; force a why when they hesitate.

B6 · Taco night list 5 min Page 2 · 06 Taco night list Optional short-path cut

Open: accordion 06 Taco night list (#act-shop)
  1. Recipe vs fridge: have / don’t have / need + quantity phrase.
  2. Check together; celebrate full chunks.

B7 · Class play 4 min Page 2 · 07 Class play Short-path cut

Open: accordion 07 Class play (#act-play) · Phrase bingo + Spin
  1. Fast path: use Spin — team says the whole phrase in 3 seconds (+ optional extra sentence).
  2. If group is large: one bingo card stamp round instead.

Block C Output + Battle ~20–25 min

Speaking output, quick writing, full Fridge Battle, then can-do exit.

C1 · Speak 6–7 min Page 2 · 08 Speak Find someone who…

Open: accordion 08 Speak (#act-speak) · Picture prompts + Find someone who…
  1. Pairs: 20-second picture prompts — partner answers with a full phrase.
  2. Then Find someone who… with quantity chunks (mingling or teacher-as-partner in 1:1).
  3. Optional bridge from countable page tab 6 Speaking if you want one more prompt set — keep it under 2 min.
Also available: Countable → tab 6 Speaking (use only if time)

C2 · Writing flash 3–4 min Page 1 · 7 Writing Short-path cut

Open: Countable → tab 7 Writing
  1. 3–4 lines about THEIR fridge: some/any + a few OR a little + one container chunk from today.
  2. You mark quantity-word wins only — celebrate.

C3 · Fridge Battle 12–14 min Page 2 · 09 Fridge Battle Full #act-battle

Open direct: food-quantifiers/#act-battle · accordion 09 Fridge Battle
  1. Full class game: two teams, five rounds, timer on. Use the page UI for names/scores.
  2. Open full screen if the projector feels tight.
  3. Teacher cue: demand whole phrases; block one-word answers.
  4. Keep energy high — this is the payoff for the whole kitchen day.
Line: “Battle rules: full chunk or no point. The fridge is listening.”

C4 · Exit / Can-do 2–3 min Page 1 · 8 Can-do

Open: Countable → tab 8 Can-do · the fridge test
  1. Tick can-dos together (name countable things; use some/any; how much/many; container phrases).
  2. One exit sentence out loud: fridge + quantity word + one chunk from Battle.

Short path ≈ 60 min Cut list

Still hits countable core + a few quantifier games + Battle. Do not skip Odd food out, Grammar hits, Practice A–B, Phrase deck, Container snap, Close call (or Memory), Speak brief, Fridge Battle, Exit.

  • Cut: Reading · Listening · Writing · Phrase wall · Memory or Close call (keep one) · Taco night list · Class play · Practice C–D if tight · Can-do tab (fold one spoken exit line instead)
  • Keep order: Odd food out → Notice → Grammar (condense hit 3) → Practice A+B (+C if time) → Phrase deck → Container snap → Close call → Speak (picture prompts only, skip Find someone who) → Fridge Battle full → 1 exit sentence
  • ~60 math: warm-up 4 · Notice 5 · Grammar 8 · Practice 8 · Deck 4 · Snap 4 · Close call 4 · Speak 4 · Battle 12–14 · Exit 2 ≈ 55–60
