Present Simple vs Continuous

Teacher Hub · dynamic class plan

Student page Teacher Hub A2.1
Teacher plan · games first

Habits vs now. Prove every form.

Warm-up races, signal-word games, three short grammar hits, trap hunt, then platform stations — Notice pairs, Analyse, Listening (After class), stretch Reading/Speaking.

≈ 55–60 min 1:1 or small group Notice → Grammar → Analyse → Listening Justify the form

Aim

Contrast Present Simple (habits · facts · permanent) with Present Continuous (now · temporary · in progress) — and explain why, not only choose the form.

  • Can-do: Talk and write about routines vs what’s happening now / this week, with clear signal words and no stative traps.
  • Materials: Student page + phone/laptop; board or chat for warm-up / trap hunt.

0 · Warm-up 5–7 min Game

Habit Sprint — activate routines before the page.

  1. Timer 40 seconds: “Name things you usually do every week — GO.” They shout / type a messy list.
  2. Second blitz (30s): “What’s happening RIGHT NOW in this room / on your phone?”
  3. Sort shout: point at a line — they yell HABIT! or NOW!
Line: “Two presents, two jobs. Simple = what’s normal. Continuous = what’s happening now or for a short time. Today we make them fight fair — and prove it.”
Optional score: +1 You for each correct shout in an 8-line blitz

1 · Vocabulary / notice 6 min Notice

Meet the contrast pairs — choral + signal flash.

Simple signals

usually · every day · on Mondays · always · sometimes · never

Continuous signals

now · look! · listen! · at the moment · today · this week

  1. Open 1 Notice. You point at Pair A–D bubbles; they chorus the bold verbs.
  2. Signal flash (oral): you say a signal word — they fire Simple! or Continuous! (12 words, no thinking time).

2 · Vocab activity 8–10 min Notice Game

Why Race + Contrast Memory.

  1. In Notice, students write short answers under each pair (a–d) — habit vs now, fact vs temporary, signal words, this week.
  2. Why Race: you read one bubble; first clean “Because…” wins +1 You.
  3. Contrast Memory (60s): hide the page. They rewrite 2 full contrasts from memory (Simple + Continuous). Reopen — check.
Page pairs: takes / is taking · lives / is living · usually cook / is cooking · works / is working

3 · Grammar presentation 1 7 min Grammar Showdown

Habits vs now — board showdown.

  1. Open 2 Grammar; focus only the two big cards first.
  2. Board:

Present Simple

habits · routines · facts · permanent
I/you/we/they + verb · he/she/it + -s/-es

Present Continuous

now · temporary · in progress
am / is / are + verb-ing

Concept duel: You say a wrong pair (Look! She takes a taxi today. / He is living in Celaya as a forever fact). They buzz and fix it. +1 You per clean fix.

4 · Grammar presentation 2 7–8 min Grammar Fire round

Signals + meaning — contrast pairs.

  • usually / every day / on Mondays → Simple
  • now / look! / listen! / at the moment → Continuous
  • today / this week / this month → often Continuous (temporary)
  • Stative: know, like, want, believe, understand → usually Simple
Fire round: You throw a cue (usually / look! / this week / know); they answer only with Simple or Continuous — 12 cues, no thinking time. Then open Grammar analysis Q1–3 briefly (works vs is working / future Continuous / I am knowing).

5 · Grammar presentation 3 5–6 min Grammar Trap hunt

Form traps + stative repairs.

  1. Preview traps orally (board or chat).
  2. Trap hunt: you read a wrong sentence; they slap the table and repair it.
  3. Quick repair race: 5 wrong lines → 5 clean rewrites.
Model traps: I am go to school. · She usually going home by bus. · Look! He take a taxi. · I am knowing the answer. · I am wanting a sandwich. · Water is boiling at 100°C. (fact → boils)

6 · Drill 12–15 min Analyse · Listening Stations

Platform practice as timed mini-games — map to student page tabs.

  1. Open the student page. Run stations:
  • 3 Analyse — 4–5 min sprint (pick 4 of 6 sentences; justify tense + signal)
  • 4 Listening · After class — play audio; A Who said it? → B Habit or now? → C T/F + evidence → D Word in context — 6–7 min
  • 5 Reading (stretch) — inference 1–2 only if time — 3 min
  • 6 Speaking (stretch) — one prompt (Contrast or Repair) — 2–3 min
  1. After each block: one miss → they say the why out loud (teacher doesn’t lecture).
  2. First-try correct answers = points for You.
Listening tip: Play the page audio first (after-class.mp3). Script button stays under the audio bar — keep it closed until Part A is done. Never “teacher will read” the script.
Cool-down: 7 Writing · Two timelines starter (3 sentences only) OR one more Speaking prompt if energy is high.

Exit ticket 2 min

  1. 3 lines about THEIR week: 1 Simple habit + 1 Continuous now/this week + 1 contrast (usually… but today…).
  2. You only mark tense / signal wins — celebrate the justification.

Timing

Warm-up 5–7 · Notice 6 · Why Race / Memory 8–10 · Grammar 1–3 ≈ 20 · Drill 12–15 · Exit 2 → ~55–60 min. Cut Reading stretch or Trap hunt repair race if short; keep Listening A–B.
