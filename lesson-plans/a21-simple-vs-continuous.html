Lesson plan · Present Simple vs Continuous

A2.1 · habits vs now · uses the live hub lesson

Open student page

≈ 55–60 min 1:1 or small group Platform: Notice → Grammar → Analyse → Listening

Lesson aim

Students can contrast Present Simple (habits · facts · permanent) with Present Continuous (now · temporary · in progress) and justify the form with signal words — not only choose answers.

  • Can-do: Speak and write routines vs what’s happening now / this week without stative traps.
  • Materials: Hub page (phone/laptop), board or chat for warm-up and trap hunt.

0 · Warm-up 5–7 min

Habit Sprint — activate routines before opening the page.

  1. 40s: “Name things you usually do every week — GO.”
  2. 30s: “What’s happening right now?”
  3. Sort shout: point at a line — HABIT! or NOW!
Teacher line: “Two presents, two jobs. Simple = what’s normal. Continuous = now or for a short time.”

1 · Vocabulary 6 min Page · Notice

Meet contrast pairs + signal words.

Simple signals

  • usually · every day
  • on Mondays · always
  • sometimes · never

Continuous signals

  • now · look! · listen!
  • at the moment · today
  • this week / this month
  1. Open 1 Notice. Chorus bold verbs in Pairs A–D.
  2. Signal flash: you say a signal — they fire Simple or Continuous.

2 · Vocab activity 8–10 min Page · Notice

Why Race + Contrast Memory.

  1. Students answer the thinking prompts under pairs a–d.
  2. Why Race: you read a bubble; first clean “Because…” wins.
  3. Contrast Memory (60s): hide page; rewrite 2 full contrasts; reopen and check.
Page pairs:
takes / is taking · lives / is living · usually cook / is cooking · works / is working

3 · Grammar presentation 1 7 min Page · Grammar

Habits vs now — two big cards.

Present Simple

habits · routines · facts · permanent

I/you/we/they + verb · he/she/it + -s/-es

Present Continuous

now · temporary · in progress

am / is / are + verb-ing

Concept check: “Look! She takes a taxi today — fix it. He is living in Celaya — forever fact or temporary?”

4 · Grammar presentation 2 7–8 min Page · Grammar

Signals + stative verbs.

  • usually / every day → Simple
  • now / look! / at the moment → Continuous
  • today / this week → often Continuous (temporary)
  • know, like, want, believe, understand → usually Simple
Micro-drill: Fire round with cues (usually / look! / this week / know). Then skim Grammar analysis Q1–3.

5 · Grammar presentation 3 5–6 min Trap hunt

Form traps + stative repairs.

  1. Trap hunt: you read a wrong sentence; they repair it aloud.
  2. Repair race: 5 wrong → 5 clean lines.
Model traps:
I am go to school. · She usually going home by bus. · Look! He take a taxi.
I am knowing the answer. · I am wanting a sandwich. · Water is boiling at 100°C. → boils

6 · Drill 12–15 min Analyse · Listening A–D

Controlled practice on the platform (fast rounds).

  1. Open student tabs as timed stations:
  • 3 Analyse — 4 of 6 sentences; justify tense + signal — 4–5 min
  • 4 Listening · After class — play audio; A Who said it → B Habit or now → C T/F + evidence → D Word in context — 6–7 min
  • 5 Reading (stretch) — inference 1–2 — 3 min
  • 6 Speaking (stretch) — Contrast or Repair prompt — 2–3 min
  1. After each block: one miss → student explains the why.
  2. Optional score: 1 point per first-try correct.
Listening tip: Play the page audio (after-class.mp3). Script button stays under the audio bar — keep it closed until Part A is done. Never “teacher will read” the script.
If time left: 7 Writing · Two timelines starter (3 sentences) or one more Speaking prompt.

Exit ticket 2 min

  1. 3 lines: 1 Simple habit + 1 Continuous now/this week + 1 contrast (usually… but today…).
  2. You only correct tense / signal errors.

Timing snapshot

  • Warm-up 5–7 · Notice 6 · Why Race / Memory 8–10
  • Grammar 1 + 2 + 3 ≈ 20 · Drill 12–15 · Exit 2
  • Total ≈ 55–60 min (cut Reading stretch or repair race if short; keep Listening A–B)
