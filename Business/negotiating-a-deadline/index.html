Grammar Anchor · First Conditional for Commitments

Use the First Conditional to state realistic future actions that depend on specific conditions.

The Structural Syntax
IF + Present Simple ➔ will / can / may / might + Base Verb

Strategic Modal Selection

Modal Executive Intent Authentic Business Sentence
will Firm commitment / Certain outcome "If the client approves the quotation today, we will reserve the carrier."
can Operational capability / Possible option "If the warehouse releases the items, we can dispatch a partial shipment."
may / might Diplomatic hedging / Uncertainty "If the supplier encounters a delay, we might need to adjust the schedule."

⚠️ Critical Fossilized Errors to Avoid

❌ "If the supplier will confirm today, we ship tomorrow." ➔ ✅ "If the supplier confirms today, we will ship tomorrow."
❌ "If the customer approved it today, we will deliver Friday." ➔ ✅ "If the customer approves it today, we will deliver Friday."
❌ "If we book the carrier now, we avoids the weekend delay." ➔ ✅ "If we book the carrier now, we will avoid the weekend delay."

Client Inquiry: Tuesday Delivery Feasibility

Listen to how the agent handles urgency without panic or premature commitment.

David Brown · Client (Green Solutions)
"Hello, this is David from Green Solutions. We need our shipment by Tuesday instead of Friday. Can you help us?"
Logistics Desk · You (GlobalTech)
"Good morning, David. I understand the urgency. Unfortunately, the warehouse is currently at peak capacity and one of our suppliers is delayed. However, I can offer a solution."
David Brown · Client
"Tuesday is very important for our assembly lines. What can you do?"
Logistics Desk · You
"If you can confirm your priority items today, we could send part of the order on Tuesday and the rest by Friday. Would that work for you?"
David Brown · Client
"Could you send two hundred units first?"
Logistics Desk · You
"Yes, if we prioritize your order, we can arrange two hundred units for Tuesday. The remaining stock would follow on Friday."
David Brown · Client
"If I accept Friday for the rest, can you offer me a small discount on transport?"
Logistics Desk · You
"Absolutely. If you accept that split schedule, we will apply a five percent courtesy discount to the full freight cost."
