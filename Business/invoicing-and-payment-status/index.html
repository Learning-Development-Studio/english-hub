Grammar Anchor · Present Perfect (Already, Yet, Just)

Use the Present Perfect to report the current status of financial tasks without needing a specific past date.

The Structural Syntax
Subject + HAVE / HAS + [already / just] + Past Participle
Questions & Negatives: Have / Has + Subject + Past Participle ... YET?

Strategic Workplace Adverbs

Adverb Meaning in AP Operations Authentic Example Sentence
ALREADY Completed sooner than expected or before now (Affirmative) "We have already received your revised invoice."
YET Not completed up to this moment, but expected (Negatives & Questions) "The finance manager hasn't signed the check yet." / "Has it cleared yet?"
JUST Completed very recently / a few moments ago (Affirmative) "I have just sent the payment confirmation to your email."

⚠️ Critical Fossilized Errors in Business Accounts

❌ "We already received the invoice yesterday." (Incorrect mix of specific past time with Present Perfect)
➔ ✅ "We received the invoice yesterday." (Simple Past) OR "We have already received the invoice." (Present Perfect)
❌ "We didn't process the payment yet."
➔ ✅ "We haven't processed the payment yet." (Use Present Perfect with 'yet')
❌ "I have just send the remittance."
➔ ✅ "I have just sent the remittance." (Must use Past Participle: send ➔ sent)

Vendor Phone Call: Invoice #4082 Payment Check

Listen to how Accounts Payable clarifies status without sounding defensive.

Claudia Morales · Apex Packaging
"Good morning. This is Claudia Morales from Apex Packaging. I'm calling to follow up on Invoice #4082 for fourteen thousand five hundred dollars. Has the payment been processed yet?"
Accounts Payable · You
"Good morning, Claudia. Let me pull up your account in our ERP system right now... Yes, I see it here. We have already received the invoice and matched it with Purchase Order PO-915."
Claudia Morales · Apex Packaging
"Great! Does that mean the payment will go out in today's run?"
Accounts Payable · You
"Not quite today. Our receiving dock hasn't signed the physical delivery receipt yet. As soon as warehouse signs off, the system will release the approval."
Claudia Morales · Apex Packaging
"I understand. Our delivery driver confirmed drop-off yesterday afternoon, so the signed slip should be ready."
Accounts Payable · You
"Perfect. I will check with the warehouse manager right after this call. If they approve it before two p.m., the payment will be included in our Thursday disbursement."
Claudia Morales · Apex Packaging
"That works well for us. Could you send me the remittance advice once the wire transfer is complete?"
Accounts Payable · You
"Absolutely, Claudia. Our automated banking portal will email you the remittance advice as soon as the funds are transferred."

AP Specialist Checklist

  • Acknowledge vendor and confirm PO number: "Let me pull up PO-915..."
  • State what has already happened: "We have already matched the PO."
  • Explain what hasn't happened yet: "The warehouse hasn't signed the slip yet."
  • Confirm the payment run day and offer remittance advice.

Remittance Confirmation Formula

"We have already scheduled your payment for the Thursday run. You will receive the remittance advice as soon as the funds are released."
