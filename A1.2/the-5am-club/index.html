Module 03 · Structural Precision

Grammar Matrix: Present Simple I (I, You, We, They)

Study the architectural structure for habitual routines. Notice that regular verbs stay in their base form, and negative routines use don't.

Grammar Architecture

Present Simple Habitual Matrix (1st & 2nd Person / Plurals)

Formula: Subject + Base Verb / don't + Base Verb
Subject Pronoun Affirmative Routine (+) Negative Routine (–) Habitual Time Marker Studio Model Exemplar
I (1st person) wake up don't wake up at 5:00 AM every day "I don't wake up at 5:00 AM."
You (2nd person) set don't set three loud alarms "You set three alarms every night."
We (1st person plural) sleep in don't sleep in on Monday mornings "We don't sleep in on Mondays."
They (3rd person plural) drink don't drink black coffee at dawn "They drink coffee before work."
Architectural Frontier Rule: When negating with don't, the main verb always remains in its clean base dictionary form (don't wake, don't drink, don't run).

Module 04 · Auditory Analysis

The Alarm War: Leo vs. Julian

Listen to the 48-second apartment confrontation between early bird Leo and night owl Julian. Answer the 6 auditory comprehension checks below.

STUDIO AUDIO TRACK 01 · 48 SECONDS

Encounter at 5:00 AM: Who Conquers the Day?

0:00 / 0:48
SFX: [Loud alarm beeps insistently: BEEP-BEEP-BEEP... Julian groans under pillow]
Leo: "Rise and shine, Julian! Five o'clock! Time to conquer the day!"
Julian: "Leo... please turn off that noise. Why do you set four alarms at dawn?"
Leo: "Because successful people wake up at five! We meditate, we drink green tea, and we run five kilometers before sunrise!"
Julian: "You run. I don't run at five in the morning. I don't even open my eyes before seven."
Leo: "Come on! You miss the best hours of the day! We have the morning club!"
Julian: "I don't care about the club, Leo. I stay up late to finish my work. I need eight hours of sleep, and I can't function without three cups of black coffee."
Leo: "Fine, sleep in! But you leave all the energy on the table!"
Julian: "I leave the energy in my bed. Turn off the light and close the door."

Auditory Comprehension · Evidence Extraction

1. What sound opens the conversation?
2. Why does Leo say successful people wake up at 5:00 AM?
3. Does Julian run at five in the morning?
4. Why does Julian stay up late at night?
5. What does Julian need before he can function?
6. What is Julian's final request to Leo?
