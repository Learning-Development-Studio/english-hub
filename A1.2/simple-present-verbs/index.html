Reading: Maya’s week

Read for detail and check meaning.

⌄

Maya lives with her brother in a small apartment. She works at a design studio and uses her laptop every day. She usually makes lunch at home, but she doesn’t cook on Fridays. Her brother studies engineering. He doesn’t work during the week, and he helps Maya with dinner. They call their parents every Sunday because their parents live in another city.

1. Maya uses a laptop for work.

2. Maya cooks every Friday.

3. Maya and her brother call their parents weekly.

Speak: Tell a partner two ways your week is similar to or different from Maya’s.
