Screen 03 · Structural Precision

Grammar Lab & -ing Spelling Rules

Master the auxiliary BE agreement and the 4 critical spelling rules of the continuous aspect.

Formula: Subject (I / You / He / We) + am / is / are (not) + Verb + -ing = Action happening RIGHT NOW
Rule 1 · General Add -ing directly.
walk → walking
sleep → sleeping
cook → cooking
Rule 2 · Silent -e Drop -e, add -ing.
drive → driving
leave → leaving
write → writing
Rule 3 · C-V-C Double Double the final consonant.
run → running
sit → sitting
stop → stopping
Rule 4 · -ie to -y Change -ie to -y.
lie (tell a lie) → lying
tie → tying
die → dying

Screen 05 · Functional Language

Spoken American Chunks

Prefabricated conversational units used across the United States to manage delays and real-time excuses.

"I'm on my way!"
Universal reply when someone asks for your current location.
"Don't worry, I'm on my way right now!"
"Be right there!"
Signals that your physical arrival will occur in moments.
"Parking the car — be right there!"
"Caught red-handed!"
Said when discovering someone in the act of lying or cheating.
"You said you were studying? Caught red-handed!"
"Yeah, right!"
Sarcastic reaction meaning "I do not believe you."
"You're driving? Yeah, right! I hear music!"
"Hold that thought!"
Polite interruption to answer another incoming call.
"Hold that thought — my boss is calling me."
"Traffic is a nightmare."
Standard spoken excuse for urban delays.
"I'm crawling at 5 mph, traffic is an absolute nightmare."
