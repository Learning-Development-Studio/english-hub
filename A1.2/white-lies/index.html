Module 03 · Structural Precision

Grammar Mechanics: Present Continuous

Study the architectural structure table, explore spelling mutations, assemble sentences from the scrambled Wordbox, and debug typical A1.2 errors.

Grammar Architecture · Structural Chart

Present Continuous Structural Matrix (Real-Time Actions)

Formula: Subject + BE (am / is / are) + Verb-ing
Person / Subject Affirmative BE (+) Negative BE (–) Action Verb (-ing) Time Marker / Complement Natural Studio Exemplar
I (1st person singular) am ('m) am not ('m not) walking out the door right now "I'm walking out the door right now."
He / She / It
Jordan, My boss, The waiter		 is ('s) is not (isn't) driving in heavy traffic at this moment "Jordan is driving in traffic right now."
We / You / They
My friends, The clients		 are ('re) are not (aren't) waiting at the restaurant for our order "They aren't waiting in the car."
RULE 1 · GENERAL Base + ing

walk → walking
wait → waiting

RULE 2 · SILENT -E Drop -e + ing

drive → driving
make → making

RULE 3 · CVC DOUBLE Double consonant

stop → stopping
run → running

RULE 4 · -IE TO -Y Change -ie to -y

lie → lying
die → dying
