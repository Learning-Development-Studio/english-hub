2. Grammar Mechanics & -ing Spelling Lab

Master the exact formula: Auxiliary BE + Main Verb in -ING.

Structural Precision
The Universal Formula (Affirmative & Negative)
Subject (I / You / He / We) + am / is / are (not) + Verb-ING = Action in Progress Right Now

The 4 Essential Spelling Rules of -ING

Rule 1: General

Just add -ing.

walk → walking
sleep → sleeping
cook → cooking

Rule 2: Silent -e

Drop the silent -e, add -ing.

drive → driving
write → writing
leave → leaving

Rule 3: CVC Double

Consonant-Vowel-Consonant: Double the last consonant.

run → running
sit → sitting
stop → stopping

Rule 4: -ie to -y

Change -ie to -y, add -ing.

lie (mentir) → lying
tie → tying
die → dying

Classroom Production

Scenario 1: The Urgent Coffee Meetup

Time: 4:30 PM · Location: Starbucks Downtown

Caller (Student A)

You have been waiting for 20 minutes at the cafe. Call Student B and ask: "Hey, where are you? What are you doing right now?" Listen for suspicious noises in the background!

Receiver (Student B - Secret Reality)

Your Secret Reality: You are eating tacos in your car while listening to loud music.

Your White Lie: Tell Student A: "I'm driving right now, traffic is crazy!"

💡 Teacher Cue: Encourage students to challenge the lie: "Wait, why are you chewing? Are you eating tacos right now?!"
