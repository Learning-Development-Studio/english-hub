Simple Present: Questions

Ask with do / does, answer with short forms (Yes, I do / No, she doesn’t), and add light WH questions. You already know affirmative and negative — now flip them into real questions.

1

Build the question pattern

do / does + base · short answers

⌄

Read the patterns aloud. Notice: after do/does, the verb stays in the base form.

Do + I / you / we / they + base?

Do you work on Saturdays?

Short answers

Yes, I do. / No, we don’t.

Does + he / she / it + base?

Does Elena start at eight?

Short answers

Yes, she does. / No, he doesn’t.

Light WH (same do/does engine)

What do you study? · Where does Omar live? · When do they finish?

Trap to avoid: Does he likes? ✗ → Does he like? ✓

Quiz · do / does accuracy

3

Fix the question

Common traps: Does he likes? / Do she…?

⌄

Type the corrected question, including the question mark.

1. Does he likes coffee?

2. Does they live in León?

3. Do she work from home?

4. What does you need?

5. Does it starts at nine?

4

Reading: Nadia’s interview day

Dialog + True/False · think, don’t guess

⌄

Nadia has a job interview on Thursday. Her brother Luca asks her questions after breakfast.

Luca: Do you know the company?
Nadia: Yes, I do. It designs apps for small shops.
Luca: Does the manager speak Spanish?
Nadia: No, she doesn’t. She speaks English and Portuguese.
Luca: What time do you start?
Nadia: The interview starts at ten. I don’t want to arrive late, so I leave home at eight fifteen.
Luca: Does Elena go with you?
Nadia: No, she doesn’t. She works downtown on Thursdays. But she texts me before I enter the building.

After the interview, Nadia calls Luca. She feels calm. The manager asks about her schedule, her English, and whether she uses design tools every week. Nadia answers carefully. She doesn’t get the job today — they call successful candidates on Friday — but she thinks the questions went well.

1. Nadia already knows something about the company.

2. The manager speaks Spanish with candidates.

3. Nadia leaves home more than an hour before the interview.

4. Elena goes to the interview with Nadia.

5. Nadia gets the job on Thursday.

6. The manager asks if Nadia uses design tools regularly.

Discuss with a partner:

1. Why does Luca ask so many questions?

2. Change Luca’s Yes/No questions into WH questions (What / Where / When).

3. What short answers does Nadia use? Make two new ones about your week.

4. Infer: Why might Nadia feel calm after the interview?

Write notes

7

Speaking: Two truths as questions

Turn facts into Do/Does questions

⌄

Write three habits about yourself as questions for your partner (two true, one false). Your partner asks you the questions; you answer with short answers. Then they guess which habit is the lie.

Useful short answers: Yes, I do. · No, I don’t. · Yes, she does. · No, he doesn’t.

Follow-ups: When do you…? Where do you…? Why don’t you…?

8

Speed ask

30-second question sprint

⌄

Your partner shows a prompt card. Ask one clean Do/Does question (or light WH) before time ends. Then swap.

PROMPT — Ask if your partner drinks tea every morning.
