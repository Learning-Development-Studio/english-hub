Interactive Communicative Simulation · B1 Level

Operation Incognito: The Diplomatic Gala

A high-stakes communicative roleplay designed to master Indirect Questions naturally through tact, discretion, and diplomatic etiquette.

The Context & Objective

You are attending the Ambassador's Annual VIP Gala. Half the room are High-Profile Guests holding confidential secrets; the other half are Undercover Agents tasked with extracting intelligence without raising suspicion.

Why Indirect Questions?

In formal diplomacy, asking direct questions ("Where is your safe?") is seen as rude, intrusive, and hostile. You must soften your inquiries to extract sensitive truth.

Simulation Mechanics

The Protocol: Two Ironclad Rules

Breaking diplomatic etiquette triggers immediate consequences in the simulation.

Rule 01: Direct Questions Are Banned

If an agent asks a direct question (e.g., "How much do you earn?" or "Did you sell the company?"):

The Diplomat's Response:
"Excuse me?! How utterly intrusive! Security, escort this person away!"
Penalty: Agent loses the intel and cannot speak to this guest again.

Rule 02: The Word-Order Trap

If the agent uses a polite starter but inverts the word order (e.g., "Could you tell me where is the key?"):

The Diplomat's Response:
"A real diplomat would never make that grammatical mistake. You are an amateur impostor!"
Penalty: Question denied.

Linguistic Architecture

The Indirect Question Blueprint

Remember: An indirect question is polite statement word order attached to a polite starter.

Polite Starter + Wh-Word / If / Whether + Subject + Verb (No Auxiliary Inversion)
Could you tell me where does he live? · Error: auxiliary 'does'
Could you tell me where he lives? · Affirmative syntax
Do you happen to know what time is it? · Error: inverted 'is it'
Do you happen to know what time it is? · Subject before verb
I was wondering did you sign the contract? · Error: missing 'if/whether'
I was wondering if you signed the contract. · Embedded clause

Agent Intel Checklist (Missions)

Agents circulate during the cocktail. Formulate your indirect inquiry before speaking.

Agent Mission Docket A
Mission 01: Find out when Ambassador Vance is leaving the gala.
Target: "Could you tell me when you plan to leave?"

Mission 02: Confirm if Lady Sterling has the vault key with her.
Target: "Do you happen to know where your vault key is?"
Agent Mission Docket B
Mission 03: Inquire why Marcus Drake dismissed his CFO.
Target: "Would you mind telling me why you fired your CFO?"

Mission 04: Check whether Ambassador Vance holds overseas assets.
Target: "I was wondering if you have an offshore account."
Perform & Reflect

The Intelligence Debrief & Wrap-Up

Gather in the briefing room. Agents present their extracted intelligence using indirect reporting.

Synthesis & Reporting Task

Each agent reports back to HQ:
"I asked Lady Sterling if she knew where the key was, and she admitted leaving it in her coat."

"I inquired whether Marcus Drake had fired his CFO, and he confirmed the termination occurred this morning."

CLT + TBLT Evaluation Rubric

E3 (Exemplary): Flawless indirect word order, zero inverted auxiliaries, natural diplomatic intonation.

E2 (Proficient): Successful information exchange with self-correction on word order.

E1 (Developing): Persistent direct question slips or auxiliary intrusion.
