Habits vs now.

Contrast routines and facts with the Present Simple, and actions happening now or for a short time with the Present Continuous. Signal words help you choose the right form.

Two presents, two jobs

Usually / every day / on Mondays → Simple. Now / look! / at the moment / today (temporary) → Continuous.
